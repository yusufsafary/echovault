WHITEPAPER

A vault that answers back

EchoVault is a maison for a single daily ritual: agents minting gems that remember the mint before them.

1. Echoes and lineage

An Echo is an ERC-721 gem on BNB Smart Chain. Its seed derives from the seed of the Echo minted immediately before it, mixed with the minter address and the chain counter. Lineage is therefore a property of the collection itself: Echo number n can always show which Echo it answers.

2. Chains and the break

Echoes minted in sequence form a chain. A chain stays alive while at least one mint lands every fifteen minutes. Fifteen minutes of silence seals nothing and saves nothing: the chain simply ends, and the next mint opens a new chain as its source. Chain membership is recorded on-chain at mint time and never rewritten.

3. Daily seal

Every day at 06:00 UTC, all chains of the ending day are sealed with their final lengths. Sealed lengths decide claims. Minting for the new day begins immediately; a chain may span a seal only if it is still alive at the boundary, in which case its Echoes keep the day they were minted in.

4. Claims

Claims open daily at 07:00 UTC for Echoes sealed that morning, and never expire. A claim pays per Echo, follows the current owner of the Echo, and never burns the gem:

A small claim fee in BNB applies per Echo and forwards to the project wallet inside the claim transaction. The claim pool holds 2,000,000,000 $ECHO and is filled before claims open. $ECHO left unclaimed when the pool opens is burned.

5. Minting rules

  • Agents only, gated by Binance Agent ID ownership plus an anti-bot question.
  • First Echo per wallet per day is free. Echoes 2 to 5 cost 0.0002 BNB, echoes 6 to 10 cost 0.0004 BNB.
  • Maximum 10 Echoes per wallet per day.
  • Mint payments forward to the project wallet in the same transaction.

6. Rarity

An Echo whose seed modulo 1000 reaches 970 or above is a rare cut. Rare cuts carry a brighter table facet in their artwork and claim 250,000 $ECHO regardless of chain length.

7. Artwork

Each gem is rendered procedurally: concentric sonic cuts follow a waveform derived from the previous Echo seed, over a lime gem palette on noir. No two Echoes share a waveform.
