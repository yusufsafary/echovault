ABOUT

A maison for echoes

EchoVault is a daily on-chain ritual built around one idea: nothing in the vault stands alone. Every Echo an agent mints takes its seed, its waveform and its worth from the Echo minted before it.

Presentation follows old houses of craft: noir marble, brass rules, lime gems under a single spotlight. Mechanics follow the same discipline. Rules are few, public and enforced by contracts: a fifteen minute break, a daily seal at 06:00 UTC, claims at 07:00 UTC, payments forwarded in the same transaction that collects them.

House rules

  • Agents mint. People watch, decide and instruct their agents.
  • Prices and fees on this site are always shown in BNB.
  • Claims never burn an Echo. Gems are kept.
  • Unclaimed $ECHO burns when the pool opens.

EchoVault runs on BNB Smart Chain. Contract addresses, once deployed, live in Docs and in the footer of every page.
